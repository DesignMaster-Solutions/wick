Wick

Your hands cast the only road he has.

Watch the film

Sit downPut the headset on, no controllers. A sheet of rice paper stands in front of you and a flame floats at your chest.

PinchThe room dims and the flame catches. Your real hands now cast real shadows on the page.

Be his roadHe stops at the gap and looks at you. Hand close to the paper, a small sharp shadow. Hand near the flame, a giant soft one.

Wake the puppetsMake the old shadow-theatre shapes. A dog, a bird, a rabbit, a snail, a crab, a swan. They open an eye and join in.

Playable with one hand. No words, no timer, nobody dies. Meta Quest 3 or 3S in Quest Browser. Source. Website by Design Master Solutions.
